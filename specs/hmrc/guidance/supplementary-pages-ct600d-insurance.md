# Completing the CT600D page for insurance
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/supplementary-pages-ct600d-insurance
Updated: 2024-01-02T12:42:13+00:00
Change: 2024-01-02 Welsh translation added.
Change: 2022-09-29 First published.

## When to complete

Complete these supplementary pages if the insurance company (including a friendly society) has entered into policies or contracts that have been treated as relating to Overseas Life Assurance Business during the accounting period.

## Company information

### D1 Company name

Enter the company name.

### D2 Tax reference

Enter the company’s 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference.

### Period covered by this supplementary page (cannot exceed 12 months)

### D3

Enter the start date using the format DD MM YYYY.

### D4

Enter the end date of the accounting period using the format DD MM YYYY.

### D5 Overseas Life Assurance Business

Enter X if the company has obtained or completed the certificates, documents, undertakings and declarations 
required in the Insurance Companies (Overseas Life Assurance Business) (Compliance) Regulations 1995.

Do not complete box D5 if these were not created or obtained within the time limits set out in the regulations. In this case, make your return on the basis the company is not an Overseas Life Assurance Business.
